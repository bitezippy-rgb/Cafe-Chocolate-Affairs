A Beautiful, Silent Getaway

Located behind MadhuSudan Law College in Cuttack, Cafe Chocolate Affairs is your perfect escape from the city noise. Our space is carefully curated with vintage mirrors, artful decor, warm fairy lights, and lots of greenery.

Whether you're looking for a peaceful meal, a spot to read, or uninterrupted space to talk with friends, our aesthetic and cozy ambiance awaits you.
